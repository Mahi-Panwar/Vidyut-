Project overview

AI-coordinated Solar + Wind + Hydro + BESS concept for Gandhi Sagar, Mandsaur (MP) · Hackathon pre-feasibility prototype

Honesty notice: this is a concept study. Items tagged Verify are from general knowledge and must be checked against official sources; Assumption items are engineering placeholders. No land ownership, wind speed, flow, head, grid capacity, approvals or incentives are claimed as confirmed. The AI Brain demo uses transparent rule-based logic, not a trained model.

Executive summary

Instead of three separate plants, the Nexus treats solar, wind, hydro and a battery as one dispatchable virtual power plant run by the Gandhi Sagar AI Energy Brain. Solar fills the day, wind covers evening/night variability, the battery shifts surplus into the evening peak, and existing hydro/reservoir flexibility (only if permitted) provides firming.

Why it is different

  • Single coordinated plant: shared grid connection, shared BESS, one dispatch brain, so lower evacuation cost and fewer curtailments.
  • Hydro-aware dispatch: reservoir constraints are hard limits in the optimiser, never assumed free.
  • Fail-safe design: edge controllers keep protection and safe-state logic if AI or comms fail.
  • Replicable: a modular design template reusable at four other MP sites by swapping terrain, water, layout and grid inputs.

Site feasibility (conditional candidate)

Verify Gandhi Sagar is a Chambal-river dam (commissioned c. 1960) on the Mandsaur–Neemuch border with an existing hydro station, commonly cited as 115 MW (5 × 23 MW), operated by MP Power Generating Co. The dam, reservoir and powerhouse are existing assets, not part of the proposal. The Gandhi Sagar Wildlife Sanctuary lies in the surrounding area, so forest and wildlife screening is critical.

🧠 AI Energy Brain — live dispatch demo

Rule-based illustration of the optimiser. In production: LSTM/XGBoost forecasts feeding a MILP/MPC dispatch solver, with edge safety interlocks.

Architecture

Sensors/SCADA → RTUs & gateways → historian → forecasting (solar, wind, reservoir, demand, price) → optimiser (battery scheduling, dispatch, curtailment minimisation) → plant controllers (inverters, turbines, hydro governors/gates, BESS PCS, grid breaker). Edge controllers hold protection and safe fallback; manual override always wins; network segmentation, RBAC, encryption, logging and IDS protect the OT layer.

Pitch outline

  1. Hook: three renewables, one brain — firm, dispatchable green power.
  2. Problem: variable renewables waste energy and miss evening peaks.
  3. Solution: hybrid plant + LFP BESS + AI Energy Brain at an existing reservoir site.
  4. Demo: AI tab (cloud-cover scenario) → 3D day/night → finance sliders.
  5. Honesty: verified vs assumed vs survey-needed, flagged throughout.
  6. Impact & scale: replicable template for four more MP sites.

Likely judge questions

  • Is hydro really available? Only as an existing-asset firming resource, subject to WRD/irrigation priority; the design works without new hydro.
  • What if AI fails? Edge controllers keep safe set-points and protection; manual override.
  • Why trust the numbers? They are labelled placeholders with a defined survey and data plan.
